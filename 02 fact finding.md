## 2.1 Interviews

### Buyer interview questions
1. How often do you shop for clothing online?
2. What frustrates you most about existing online clothing stores?
3. How important is being able to see reviews before buying?
4. Would you want to track your order status after purchase? How?
5. What would make you trust a small/independent boutique seller online?

### Vendor interview questions
1. What challenges do you face listing and managing products online currently?
2. How do you currently handle orders and inventory?
3. What information do you wish customers could see about your products?
4. How important is it to manage multiple products/variants (size, color) easily?
